I used call-preserved registers %rbx, %r12, %r13, %r14 to hold the partial results x, a, b, and c because they need to survive across multiple calls to f. I then used a non-preserved register %rcx to temporarily stage a value before one of the f calls, pushing it onto the stack beforehand and popping it after to preserve it across the call since f zeros all non-preserved registers.
